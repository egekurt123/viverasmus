WHY VIVEERASMUS

Because moving abroad is a big enough adventure already.

When you arrive somewhere new, even simple things can take extra effort. Where should you look? What does the rent include? Is the place ready for a student stay?

We’re building viverasmus to make those first steps easier to understand. One place to explore homes, compare the monthly details and get a feel for Sevilla before you arrive.
