WHY VIVEERASMUS

Because moving abroad is a big enough adventure already.

We’re building viverasmus to make those first steps easier to understand. One place to explore homes, compare the monthly details and get a feel for Sevilla before you arrive.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few things you might be wondering.

Still have a question? We’re happy to help you find your way around.

Ask us anything ↗
How do I find a place that suits me?

Browse the homes in Sevilla and narrow your search by neighborhood, monthly rent, move-in date and the kind of place you’re looking for.

How does the renting process work?

Browse homes and use the filters to find places that fit your plans. Review the rent, amenities and what’s included, then contact the viverasmus team when a home feels right. You can ask questions and start the next step with the team.

What’s included in an apartment?

It depends on the home. Listings show whether a place is furnished, whether utilities are included, and its amenities, which can include Wi-Fi, air conditioning, heating, a washing machine, a dishwasher, a balcony or an elevator. Cleaning service may also be available; contact the team to confirm the details for a specific apartment.

Are the prices shown final?

Prices are approximate. The final price will be confirmed after booking, so get in touch with the team if you’d like to clarify a listing.

Can I ask about a specific listing?

Of course. Use the contact page to tell us which home you’re interested in and what you’d like to know.

Can I get help learning about Sevilla?

Yes. Our listings and local details are here to help you explore neighborhoods and start getting a feel for the city before you arrive.
